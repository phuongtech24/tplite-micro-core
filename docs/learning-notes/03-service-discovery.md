# 03 - Service Discovery

## Hoc truoc khi code

Service Discovery la noi cac service dang ky ten va dia chi hien tai cua minh.

Thay vi hard-code `localhost:8083`, service co the goi theo ten nhu `account-service`.

## Cau nho phong van

Discovery Server giai quyet viec service co the doi port/instance nhung cac service khac van tim duoc thong qua service name.
