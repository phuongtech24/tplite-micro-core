# 01 - Microservice Overview

## Hoc truoc khi code

Microservice la cach chia he thong lon thanh nhieu service nho, moi service nam mot nghiep vu rieng, co the deploy rieng va scale rieng.

Trong du an nay, minh chon 5 service:

```text
identity-service
customer-service
account-service
transfer-service
notification-service
```

## Vi sao khong tach qua nhieu?

De hoc chac truoc. Neu tach loan, card, audit ngay tu dau thi de roi vao phuc tap ha tang thay vi hieu ban chat.

## Diem can nho phong van

Microservice giup tach domain va scale doc lap, nhung doi lai phuc tap hon ve transaction, network, retry, logging va monitoring. Vi vay v1 chi tach cac module co bien gioi ro rang.
